Brief

What the client needed

Not just to talk about their services, but to express the core values: expertise, decades of experience, and a human approach to clients.

Attorneys Judith Vees and Berthold Guardiera needed a website that would fully represent their firm online. The firm specialises in six key areas of law: family, inheritance, labour, medical, corporate, and tort law. Each of them required a separate, detailed page.

It was important to show that in a small firm, the client receives a personalised approach — unlike large, faceless practices. And to encourage visitors to take action: book a consultation right from the first visit.

02 / 03
Approach

What I did

01

Structure across six practice areas

Built a website where each of the six practice areas is a standalone section with its own detailed description. This lets visitors find the information they need quickly and builds trust through demonstrable specialisation.

02

The attorneys' profiles

Designed the content and presentation around the attorneys themselves. The homepage and dedicated blocks detail their education, experience, and specialisations: Judith Vees — in practice since 1993, family law specialist since 1996, and a certified mediator. Berthold Guardiera — in practice since 1982, labour and medical law specialist. This is a strong signal of reliability for potential clients.

03

Fixed consultation fee

Implemented a clear call to action: a fixed fee for an initial consultation — 190 EUR net. This removes the uncertainty barrier and encourages visitors to book.

04

Maps and contact form

Integrated Google Maps to display the firm's location in Rottweil, and set up a contact form for consultation bookings. Enquiries go directly to the firm's inbox.

05

Full GDPR / DSGVO compliance

Ensured full compliance with the European regulation: proper cookie notices, consent management for data processing, and reCAPTCHA integration to protect forms from spam. The website also includes a legally required Impressum section per German law.
