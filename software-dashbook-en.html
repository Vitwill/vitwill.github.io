About

What it is and who it's for

One tool instead of six apps: from a to-do list to a creative diary, with an AI assistant that helps you understand yourself.

DashBook is a web application for those tired of switching between notes, calendars, task managers, birthday reminders, and diaries. I've gathered six independent scenarios into a single interface — each with its own colour coding so it's easy to orient yourself inside the app.

A separate layer covers the delicate and creative moments: a diary with the ability to upload photos and draw right on top of them, automatic countdown to upcoming birthdays, and email notifications about upcoming meetings and events.

And above all of it — the AI assistant. It doesn't just execute commands: when needed, it helps you work through a problem or cope with stress. This turns DashBook from a regular organiser into a space for taking care of yourself.

Tabs

Six scenarios

✅ Tasks

A classic to-do list: write down tasks, tick them off, delete what's no longer needed. At the top — a progress bar showing how many are completed out of the total.

📝 Notes

A space for quick thoughts and ideas not tied to a specific date. Notes appear as sleek sticker-style cards with an exact timestamp. Perfect for shopping lists or sudden insights.

🤝 Meetings

A business calendar: plan meetings with title, date, exact time, and location. All meetings are automatically sorted by date — you always see where and when your next event takes place.

✨ Events

A section for important life moments and celebrations. Add dates with a detailed description of what's happening. Each event is highlighted with a large date marker — month and day.

🎂 Birthdays

A special tracker for celebrations. Add the names of loved ones and their birthdays — the app calculates on its own how many days remain until the holiday. If a birthday is today, you're greeted with celebratory confetti and a cake icon.

📖 Diary

A personal space for memories. Write detailed entries about how the day went, switching between dates in the calendar. Upload photos (by drag-and-drop) and draw directly in the app or on top of your photos.

AI assistant

What else is inside

01

AI assistant with wellness support

The built-in AI helper executes commands, but its main role is to be there when you need it. If stress has built up or there's a problem that's hard to sort out alone, the assistant helps lay the situation out and suggests a step you can take right now. This turns DashBook into a quiet space for self-care, not just another to-do list.

02

Voice input

Talking to the AI assistant and filling in fields is available by voice. Especially handy when your hands are busy or you just don't feel like typing — dictate a thought or a task and it lands where it needs to.

03

Four themes

Pink/Ocean, Ocean, Midnight, and Crystal. Themes change instantly, the setting persists between sessions — tuned for different times of day and moods.

04

Email notifications about events

The app automatically checks the "Meetings" and "Birthdays" tabs. If an event falls on the current date — the system sends a notification to the address from your profile. In the demo version, notifications are logged to the developer console — real email delivery would require connecting a mail service.

05

Live chat

A built-in chat for quick communication. Adjustable font size, choice of font (Roboto by default) — for those who spend a lot of time in conversations.

06

Multilingual interface

Switch languages on the fly. Suitable for working across different markets and for users who write notes in several languages.
