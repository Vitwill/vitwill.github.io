About

What it is and who it's for

Finding promising YouTube channels by niche and region — with an estimate of activity, publishing pace, and potential revenue.

YouTube Analyzer Pro is a tool for those exploring new niches, evaluating competitors, or researching the YouTube market. The app works in real time based on official YouTube Data API v3 data and lets you find promising channels in selected niches and geographic regions, estimate their activity, publishing pace, and potential revenue from monetisation.

The audience is content creators, producers, marketers, and niche researchers. For those launching a channel, planning a content series, or looking for a direction with the least competition and the greatest potential.

Features

What it can do

01

Flexible search and filtering

Search by keywords — refine your query with arbitrary phrases (e.g., "games", "cooking", "renovation"). 12 ready-made popular niches — from personal finance to IT and esports (full list below).

02

Multilingual localisation and regions

Support for search across 12 countries (USA, Germany, France, UK, Japan, South Korea, Russia, Brazil, Canada, Spain, Italy, Australia) and 4 macro-regions (Americas, Europe, Asia, Africa). Automatic query translation: the algorithm translates niche terms into the target country's language — it searches in Japanese for Japan, in German for Germany, in Portuguese for Brazil. This lets you discover real local channels without manual translation.

03

Channel table and one-click sorting

Search results displayed as an interactive table with a preview, channel name, subscriber count, and estimated revenue. One-click sorting by name, subscriber count, and estimated revenue (ascending / descending).

04

Deep channel analysis panel

Clicking the "Analyse" button triggers a detailed scan of the selected channel: country of registration, exact subscriber count and high-resolution channel avatar, video upload frequency (from "Daily" to "Once a month or less"), revenue from the latest video based on the last video's views, monthly revenue ranges — minimum and maximum projected monthly earnings, and quick jump — a button to open the channel directly on YouTube in a new tab.

Deployment

Three usage formats

01 / 03

Web application (Cloud)

Runs right in the browser by link. No installation required — open it any time, from any device.

02 / 03

Standalone Windows app

Built on Electron. No third-party libraries required — launches with a single click as a fully independent program. Portable .exe format.

03 / 03

Single-file HTML

Using the vite-plugin-singlefile plugin, the project is bundled into a single YouTube_Analyzer.html file that combines all code, scripts, and styles with no external dependencies.
