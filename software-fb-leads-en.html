About

What it is and who it's for

A full lead lifecycle: from automatic import from ad sources — to conversion analytics by channel.

FB Leads is a specialised CRM system for teams working with a high volume of inbound leads from advertising. The app covers the entire lead lifecycle: it collects data from Meta Lead Ads, Google Sheets, and Excel/CSV files, validates contacts, scores lead quality through VIP scoring, and moves each deal through the pipeline to its final status.

The core goal is to free managers from manual work with duplicates, lost contacts, and scattered sources. The app automatically finds duplicates, prioritises the most valuable leads, and shows where to focus attention first. It runs as a web application in any modern browser and can also be packaged as a standalone Portable .exe for Windows.

Modules

Six systems

01 / 06

Authentication and profiles

A multi-profile system with independent manager and administrator accounts. Dual verification via Email + SMS — on registration, the system generates 6-digit confirmation codes. An integrated virtual message gateway lets you view and auto-fill verification codes directly in the interface.

02 / 06

Intelligent lead scoring

VIP scoring: automatic identification of the most valuable clients by key lead parameters — budget size, urgency, region, contact completeness. Automatic deduplication prevents repeated processing of duplicates by matching phone and email. Colour-coded priorities split leads into critical, standard, and archived.

03 / 06

Integrations and sources

Import data from Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV files. Direct synchronisation with remote Google Workspace sheets by link. Integration with Meta Lead Ads (Facebook / Instagram) via webhooks with validation of EAA access tokens and form IDs. Instant export of the leads base with filters back to Excel.

04 / 06

Deal management (Pipeline)

Pipeline status model: New → In progress → Qualified → Won → Lost. A lead card with detailed view and editing: contact history, manager notes, assigned owner, tags. Multi-level filtering by source, status, dates, and text search by name or phone.

05 / 06

Analytics and reporting

Conversion funnel with visualisation at every processing stage. Source distribution — a chart of ad channel performance. Response speed metrics — average time to first contact by a manager. Audit Trail — a transparent chronicle of all operations, statuses, and imports.

06 / 06

Notifications and ergonomics

Push and sound notifications when critical VIP leads arrive. Bilingual interface — fast switching between English and Russian. Cross-platform: runs in any modern browser and packages as a standalone Portable .exe for Windows.
