Brief

What the client needed

Bring a huge product range — from gourmet oils to functional omega-3 blends — into one digital space, staying clear and inspiring.

Bio Planète is the first organic oil mill in Europe, founded in 1984 in the southwest of France. Today it's a Franco-German family business with ~160 employees producing over 70 cold-pressed oils.

The website had to solve several tasks at once: present the brand as a leader in organic oils in German and French retail, express the philosophy "Food changes the world" (#ESSENVERÄNDERTDIEWELT), sell products through its own online shop, and — most importantly — show consumers that good food is not only a pleasure but also a concern for their health and the climate.

Approach

What I did

01

A multi-purpose website

Built a website that combines several key directions: an e-commerce store with over 70 cold-pressed organic oils, an educational portal with a knowledge base on nutrition and oils, and a section with recipes and culinary inspiration.

02

The signature "Flavour Worlds" (Genusswelten)

Integrated a structure where products are organized by category: gourmet oils, aromatic seasonings, and functional omega-3 blends. This turns shopping from a simple product selection into a culinary journey.

03

Educational content

Implemented unique materials: a section on the basics of omega-3 and omega-6, 10 facts about olive oil, a guide to tasting oils, and an interactive quiz "Which olive oil suits me?". This positions the brand not just as a producer, but as an expert and mentor in conscious eating.

04

Visual expression of values

Designed the visual and textual presentation to reflect the company's key values: taste, quality, health, involvement, and inspiration. The site expresses the philosophy that good food is "everyday heroes" that bring pleasure, strengthen health, and care for the climate.

05

Social responsibility

Added a section with stories about the company's initiatives: participation in the "Bio-Brotbox" program for schoolchildren, support for healthy soils, and sustainable organic farming.

06

Brand innovations

Highlighted the company's innovativeness: the site tells about revolutionary developments, including Europe's first reusable packaging for vegetable oil and a unique 3D process for producing an especially mild flaxseed oil.

07

Responsiveness and integrations

Ensured a responsive design and fast loading, as well as integration with online ordering and delivery systems for international sales.
